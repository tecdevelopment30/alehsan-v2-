A development company built to move projects from concept to completion.

Alehsan International Development Co. (AID) is a mid-sized, financially strong company with 100% GCC-owned equity, registered in the Kingdom of Bahrain since 2006. Its business history traces back to Abu Dhabi in 1968, and AID remains one of a select group of professional engineering contractors contributing continuously to the region's construction and development sectors.

Since 2006 AID has completed numerous construction, trading, and oil & gas engineering projects for public and private clients across civil, marine, oil & gas and MEP works. Today that track record extends into Artificial Intelligence & Automation, Real Estate development, and Robotics & Mechatronics, carried out with the same discipline.

A wide network of vendors and strategic partners across the GCC, France, Norway, the UK and Germany lets AID meet each client's specific engineering requirements, onshore or offshore, or through complete contracts delivered with its international partners.
