Our History

A development company built to move projects from concept to completion.

Alehsan International Development Co. (AID) is a mid-sized, financially strong company with 100% GCC-owned equity, registered in the Kingdom of Bahrain since 2006, with a business history that traces back to Abu Dhabi in 1968.

Since 2006 AID has completed numerous construction, trading, and oil & gas engineering projects for public and private clients across civil, marine, oil & gas and MEP works. Today that track record extends into AI & automation, real estate, interior design, data and digital solutions, and robotics & mechatronics, delivered with the same discipline.

A network of vendors and strategic partners across the GCC, France, Norway, the UK and Germany lets AID meet each client's specific engineering requirements, onshore or offshore, or through complete contracts with its international partners.
