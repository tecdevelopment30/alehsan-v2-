A development company built to move projects from concept to completion.

ALehsan grew from a family business with generations of roots in Arabia, tracing back to a trading enterprise started in the UAE in the 1970s. In the early 1990s, Al Majdal Contracting & Trading Company was founded in Abu Dhabi, and in 2010, ALehsan International Company was established in Bahrain, carrying that legacy into a new regional chapter.

Since then, ALehsan has grown steadily into a multidisciplinary development company rooted in trusted execution and long-term value creation. We combine project leadership, operational discipline, market access, and technical delivery to move ventures forward with clarity from planning through realization.

Our work spans project management, construction contracting, steel galvanization, mechanical engineering, AI and automation systems, and real estate development — enabling clients and partners to execute across the broader MENA landscape.
